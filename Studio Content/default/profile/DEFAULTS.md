# Starting defaults

## Creative

- Audience: curious people who want useful ideas explained clearly; refine per video.
- Voice: direct, warm, lively, and plain-spoken. Keep the speaker's personality; avoid corporate filler and fake certainty.
- Story: one central idea, a subject-specific hook, a concrete example or demonstration, and a clear payoff.
- Visuals: polished editorial design with purposeful changes in framing and motion. Prefer original examples, diagrams and real details over decorative stock imagery.
- Accessibility: caption spoken words, protect safe areas, use strong contrast, and check text at phone size.
- Branding: the current JStudio wordmark and palette are local starter assets, not an approved personal or corporate identity. Do not add the wordmark to a public video by default.

## Production

- New-project frame: 1920×1080 landscape (16:9).
- Frame rate: use the source or intended native rate; common starting points are 24, 25 or 30 fps.
- Make alternate aspect ratios only when the platform or story benefits. Reframe and re-layout important elements for each version.
- Use Animator for newly authored animation and Editor for editing, captions, sound and export.
- Voice, music, runtime, pacing, and final variants: decide from the brief, not a rigid preset.
- Local AI narration: unavailable on this Mac under its current native-library security policy. Use a recorded or externally generated voice track when needed.

These are starting points, not constraints. A video's brief and its selected brand pack decide its final treatment.
