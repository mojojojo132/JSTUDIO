# Platform export guide

Checked 2026-10-12. Platform limits change; recheck the linked official help pages before an export when a project depends on a specific limit.

## YouTube

- Standard master: 16:9, 1920×1080. Use 3840×2160 only when the source and edit support genuine 4K detail.
- Export a progressive MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio at 48 kHz. Keep the source frame rate; YouTube lists 24, 25, 30, 48, 50 and 60 fps as common rates.
- YouTube's SDR recommendation is BT.709. Its upload guidance recommends 8 Mbps for 1080p at standard frame rates and 12 Mbps at high frame rates.
- Do not bake black bars into the picture. The player adapts to vertical and square videos; choose those shapes for deliberate Shorts or cutdown versions.

Official references: [YouTube recommended upload encoding settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171) and [video resolution and aspect ratios](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6375112).

## LinkedIn organic posts

- LinkedIn currently accepts 256×144 through 4096×2304, aspect ratios from 1:2.4 through 2.4:1, 10–60 fps, and videos from 3 seconds to 15 minutes on desktop. Maximum file size is 5 GB.
- Default to the 16:9 master for a full explanation. A 4:5 or 9:16 recut can use more of the phone feed for a short, single-idea post; keep titles and key action away from the edges and platform controls.
- A short native cut is a useful creative starting point, not a platform limit. LinkedIn's sharing guide suggests 30 seconds to 2 minutes as a guide; let the idea set the final length.
- Upload captions and a custom thumbnail when useful. Check how the video and its safe areas appear in the feed before posting.

Official references: [Share videos on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a7174587) and [LinkedIn Sharing Guide](https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/help/linkedin/en-us/LinkedIn-Sharing-Guide.pdf).

Paid placements have their own requirements. Check LinkedIn's [video ad specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a424737) separately before creating an ad.

## Deliverables per video

- Keep one complete 16:9 master as the canonical version unless the brief says otherwise.
- Add only useful platform edits: for example, a 30–120 second LinkedIn cut, a vertical short, or a square excerpt. Give each its own opening and ending if the story needs them.
- Name exports with topic, platform, aspect ratio and version, such as `topic-youtube-16x9-v1.mp4`.
- Retain the project source, final encoded candidate, caption file when available, thumbnail, and approved platform copy together.
