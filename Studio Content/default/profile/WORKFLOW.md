# Studio workflow

Create one project per finished video under `Studio Content/default/projects/`. Keep that video's brief, sources, media, editable animation, native edit, review versions and exports together. Put only genuinely reusable assets and templates in `Studio Content/default/library/`.

## Make the video

1. **Choose the viewer's payoff.** Name the intended audience, the question they have, and the one thing they should understand, feel or do afterward.
2. **Find the story.** Pick a specific example, demonstration, comparison, experiment or human moment that makes the idea worth watching. Start with a hook that belongs to this subject; avoid generic introductions.
3. **Build the explanation.** Move from curiosity to context, evidence or demonstration, useful takeaway, and a satisfying close. Pair spoken lines with what viewers see. Use a real example to teach; never invent proof.
4. **Design the visuals and sound.** Choose the simplest visual treatment that makes the idea clear and memorable. Use Animator for newly authored animation; use Editor for edit, timing, captions, audio and export. Keep narration natural, captions readable, and music subordinate to speech.
5. **Edit for each destination.** Make the YouTube version the complete story. Create a LinkedIn cutdown or alternate framing only when it works as a complete, native-feeling post; recompose vertical or square versions rather than blindly cropping the master.
6. **Check the complete candidate.** Review the encoded picture, story, facts, captions, sound, mobile readability and platform framing. Fix specific defects, then request one decision on the complete candidate. Preserve review versions and evidence in the project.
7. **Deliver and post deliberately.** Deliver the exact approved file. Publishing to YouTube or LinkedIn is a separate action and requires a direct request.

## Creative bar

- **Entertaining:** build curiosity, contrast, surprise, warmth or humor into the subject. A joke is optional; viewer interest is not.
- **Educational:** show where to start, what happens, why it matters, and how the viewer can recognize or use the result.
- **Beautiful:** make framing, light, color, typography, motion and sound feel considered. Readability and meaning lead; effects serve them.
- Keep the pacing lively but give important ideas room to land. Let strong moments breathe.
- Do not force a talking-head format, a 3D look, constant motion, sound effects, or one recurring scene template. Choose the form that best tells this story.
- Treat `jstudio/1.0.0` as a local starter identity, not a personal logo or official corporate identity. Keep its wordmark off published work unless the project explicitly calls for it; use supplied, approved personal or organizational identity when available.

## Working files

- Start from `Studio Content/default/library/templates/VIDEO-BRIEF.md` and `SCRIPT-STORYBOARD.md` for substantial new videos.
- Use `profile/CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md` for the standing visual and writing direction and `profile/PLATFORM-EXPORTS.md` for current export targets.
- Frame rate, runtime, voice, music, visual treatment and final variants follow the actual brief. Local AI narration is currently unavailable; use a recorded or separately generated voice track when narration is wanted.
- Retain source links and rights notes for factual claims and external media. Keep the complete-candidate review and delivery records in the project.
