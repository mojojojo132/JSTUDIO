# Complete-candidate checklist

Use this against the encoded candidate, not just the timeline.

## Story and accuracy

- [ ] The opening earns attention quickly and fits the subject.
- [ ] The video teaches one clear takeaway with a concrete example or demonstration.
- [ ] Claims, names, numbers and on-screen details match their sources.
- [ ] The close delivers the promise and feels complete.

## Picture and sound

- [ ] Framing, color, type and motion feel intentional and coherent.
- [ ] Text and important action remain readable on a phone and clear of platform controls.
- [ ] Captions are accurate, well-timed and easy to read.
- [ ] Speech is clear; music and effects support rather than compete with it.
- [ ] No accidental black bars, clipping, flash frames, missing media or abrupt audio endings.

## Platform files

- [ ] Correct version, aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate and file name.
- [ ] Thumbnail and post copy are ready when needed.
- [ ] Each cutdown stands alone and has been reframed for its destination.
- [ ] Current platform limits were checked for this output.

## Review and handoff

- [ ] Complete encoded candidate reviewed; remaining limitations are recorded honestly.
- [ ] User decision recorded against the exact candidate.
- [ ] Delivered file matches the approved candidate.
- [ ] Posting is done only when directly requested.
