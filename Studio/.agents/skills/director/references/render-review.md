# Review the exact render

Use [production checks and the local review workspace](../../../../runtime/PRODUCTION-CHECKS.md) to prepare an exact-candidate browser review, record timecoded findings and save a compatible receipt. `workflow qc` and `workflow captions` produce independent technical evidence before perceptual review. The workbench never infers declarations from player events and provides no approval action.

Use `npm run workflow -- <slug> review assessment.json` to preserve a render-review receipt. Assessment paths resolve from the project. New assessments use schemaVersion 2 and include `media` (project-relative encoded file), `durationSeconds` (measured duration), `reviewer`, `inspectedRanges` (`[{"start":0,"end":30}]` in seconds), `displayTarget` (`{"context":"Mobile video player","minimumWidthPx":390}`), `legibilityInspection`, `playback` and `listening` status objects, `defects`, and optional notes. A defect records severity (critical/high/medium/low) and description. The helper computes the actual file SHA-256 and preserves each receipt separately. Existing schemaVersion 1 receipts remain readable; new receipts must declare the display target.

`legibilityInspection` is `{"status":"complete","inspectedWidthPx":390,"checks":[{"timeSeconds":12.5,"text":"Required claim or disclaimer","result":"readable","evidence":"Inspected the exact encoded frame at 390px wide."}]}` when the required text has been checked at the declared target width. Include every required claim and disclaimer that matters to legibility, especially small support copy. Each check time must fall inside `inspectedRanges`. Use `status:"partial"` with at least one checked frame and a reason, `status:"unavailable"` with `inspectedWidthPx:null`, no checks and an honest reason, or `status:"not-applicable"` with no checks and a reason when no on-screen or caption text is in scope. An assessed `legibilityAccessibility` score requires complete target-width checks; otherwise leave it unassessed. New receipts count perception as complete only when full playback/listening and target-width legibility are complete or legitimately not applicable. Widths are rendered player widths in pixels, not encoded master dimensions or a guarantee of actual device physical size.

## Review the finished film in distinct passes

For the complete encoded candidate, use the same exact file and SHA-256 for each pass. When available, perform these checks in addition to a normal, uninterrupted full-speed watch:

1. **Normal playback:** watch the whole candidate with picture and sound to judge the story, pacing, continuity and overall experience. Do not pause to inspect production details during this pass.
2. **Muted picture:** replay without sound. Check that the images, actions and on-screen words still make the central visual explanation understandable; note where narration is carrying information the picture should show.
3. **Audio only:** listen to the whole candidate without watching the image. Check whether speech is clear and the spoken story holds together, and whether music or effects distract from it. For a deliberately silent film, record listening as not applicable with the reason.
4. **Phone-size with captions:** view the encoded candidate at the declared minimum player width with captions enabled. Inspect the densest captions and the most important labels, diagrams and transformations, including transitions and platform-control safe areas. Record timestamped legibility checks rather than judging from the full-size master.

Use `notes` to say which passes were completed, partially completed or unavailable, and why. `playback`, `listening`, `inspectedRanges` and `legibilityInspection` retain their existing meanings; the extra passes do not create new schema fields or approval gates. Do not describe a muted pass as full playback, a phone-size spot check as full-film playback, or technical analysis as listening. Scale extra sampling to the film's risk and complexity, but disclose any omitted pass and do not score a dimension whose evidence was not observed.

Playback status is performed, unavailable or not-performed. Listening additionally permits not-applicable for demonstrably silent output. Every non-performed status requires a reason. Record actual coverage: sparse frames do not establish full temporal review, and decode success never establishes listening. Use playback when available; otherwise inspect time-indexed frames across the full duration with denser coverage around transitions and visibly disclose the unsupported playback/listening capability. Ranges describe the time spans actually inspected, not simply the positions of the first and last sampled frame. Keep sample spacing and exact evidence paths in notes. Do not claim continuous review from a frame strip.

Before seeking complete-candidate approval (or an explicitly requested intermediate decision), inspect the latest exact encoded artifact, including rerenders after fixes. Record actual coverage, defects and affected-range rechecks. Approval evidence records the actual user statement and may link the exact-media review receipt; supplied receipts are verified. Honest incomplete/unavailable perception is retained separately; it does not require an additional user attestation to record creative approval. Do not omit available QA or convert technical checks into watched/listened claims.

Use `workflow gate final` before presenting the default complete-candidate decision. Use the animation/storyboard reports only for requested intermediate reviews. Reports supplement review and create no approval. Guarded review-only imports permit candidate assembly without source-clip approval.

## Coverage and rechecks

Use optional `inspectionMethod` (`sampled-frames`, `continuous-playback`, or `mixed`). `declaredRangeCoverageComplete` describes supplied ranges only; `continuousPlaybackComplete` and `perceptualReviewComplete` are separate. Legacy `coverageComplete` remains a declared-range alias, never proof of playback. Full-perception verification remains a distinct claim requiring actual playback/listening. Creative approval accepts an unambiguous actual decision such as “Approved” without asserting that claim or requiring a separate attestation.

Defects may include `id` and `status` (`open`, `resolved`, `accepted`). A resolved defect requires `verification: {mediaSha256, note}` matching the current exact media and explaining the recheck. Legacy descriptions have no inferred resolution. Accepted is an explicit recorded disposition, not automatic user approval.

New assessments should include the structured `qualityAssessment` scorecard described in [continuous improvement](continuous-improvement.md). Its eight dimensions require evidence; whole-film pacing/story/cohesion need playback, and sound needs perceptual listening. Mark unsupported dimensions unassessed. Scores are diagnostic signals, not pass/fail thresholds or approval evidence.

Each `presented-for-review` gate automatically creates a hash-bound learning packet and findings template beside the gate report. Analyze its review coverage, defects, command failures and actual token/cost coverage; then record film-only versus reusable Studio/creative-system findings through `npm run improve`. See [continuous improvement](continuous-improvement.md). The shared profile registry retains only generalized findings and coverage summaries.
